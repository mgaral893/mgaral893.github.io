Privacy Policy — Fit Passport

Package: com.mgaral893.fitpassport
Last updated: 16 July 2026

Summary: Fit Passport works on your device. It does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics or a backend. Garment information remains in the app's private local storage.

1. Controller and contact

The controller is the independent developer mgaral893. Privacy questions: mgaral893@gmail.com.

2. Information stored locally

The app stores garment records that you enter: brand, optional store, category, size, fit result and creation date. This information is held in the app's private database on your device.

3. Photos and on-device text recognition

If you choose to scan a label, Fit Passport sends the capture request to the system camera or photo picker. Text recognition runs on-device using a bundled ML Kit model. A camera capture is held temporarily in the app cache and deleted after processing or cancellation. The source image is not uploaded or retained as part of the garment record.

4. Data collection and sharing

  • No account, name, email, location or contacts are requested.
  • No personal data is transmitted to the developer.
  • No advertising or analytics SDK is included.
  • No information is sold or shared with third parties.
  • The app does not request Internet, Camera, storage or Advertising ID permission. System camera/photo-picker apps operate under their own permissions and policies.

5. Retention and deletion

Records remain until you delete them, clear the app's storage or uninstall Fit Passport. Version 1.0 disables cloud backup. Removing app data removes the local database.

6. Children

Fit Passport is a general clothing utility and is not directed specifically at children under 13. It does not request names, profiles or body measurements.

7. Security

Data is stored inside Android's private application sandbox. No system can guarantee absolute security, but the app reduces exposure by avoiding accounts, network transfer and persistent source photos.

8. Your choices and rights

You control the information entered and can delete individual records or all app data. Because the developer does not receive the local database, requests for server-side access or deletion generally do not apply.

9. Changes

This policy will be updated before adding any material data collection, cloud service, advertising or analytics. The date above identifies the current version.

Política de privacidad — Fit Passport

Paquete: com.mgaral893.fitpassport
Última actualización: 16 de julio de 2026

Resumen: Fit Passport funciona en tu dispositivo. No requiere cuenta ni incluye publicidad, analítica o backend. La información de prendas permanece en el almacenamiento privado local de la app.

El responsable es el desarrollador independiente mgaral893. Contacto: mgaral893@gmail.com.

La app guarda los datos que introduces: marca, tienda opcional, categoría, talla, resultado de ajuste y fecha. Permanecen en la base de datos privada del dispositivo.

5. Conservación y eliminación

Los registros permanecen hasta que los eliminas, borras los datos de la aplicación o desinstalas Fit Passport. La versión 1.0 desactiva las copias cloud.

6. Menores

Es una utilidad general de ropa y no está dirigida específicamente a menores de 13 años. No solicita nombres, perfiles o medidas corporales.

7. Seguridad y derechos

Los datos se guardan en el sandbox privado de Android. Puedes borrar registros individuales o todos los datos desde los ajustes del sistema.
